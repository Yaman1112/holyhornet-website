Perching UAV: attach, switch off, keep watching

A drone that finds a surface it can hold on to, attaches with switchable magnets or a bar claw, proves the grip will hold, then switches its motors off and works as a sensor station for hours. Before it leaves it checks battery, wind, airspace, motors and the release; if anything fails, it stays attached and reports.

  • 238 W hovering → 3.3 W perched
  • 45 simulated test missions, 0 crashes
  • 10 scenarios with fault injection

Counter-UAS · Interactive simulation

SkyShield: see every drone, touch nothing

A passive drone detection and tracking system for protected sites. It fuses radar, RF, Remote ID, camera and acoustic sensors into one airspace picture, classifies each track, and warns the operator about approaches, loitering and zone intrusions. It observes only: no jamming, no spoofing, no interception.

  • 5 sensor types fused
  • 8 scenarios to run
  • Detection only

Runs entirely in your browser, nothing to install. A development prototype: the sensors are simulated and no field performance has been measured yet.

Autonomous UAV Lab: see, map, localise, fly

A quadrotor that enters an unknown 3D world, builds a map from its own LiDAR and camera, estimates where it is, plans a safe route and flies it. Watch the map grow and the estimate track the truth, change the drone, its sensors and its controller, and send it new goals.

  • 3D EKF-SLAM with known and new landmarks
  • Minimum-snap trajectories + LQR
  • 11 laboratories · 15 test scenarios
