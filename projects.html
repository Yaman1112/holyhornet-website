SkyShield: see every drone, touch nothing

A passive drone detection and tracking system for protected sites. It fuses radar, RF, Remote ID, camera and acoustic sensors into one airspace picture, classifies each track, and warns the operator about approaches, loitering and zone intrusions. It observes only: no jamming, no spoofing, no interception.

  • 5 sensor types fused
  • 8 scenarios to run
  • Detection only

Moving platforms: launch from, and land on, vehicles and ships

Drones that take off from a moving truck, vessel or robot, fly a mission, find the platform again and land on its pad while it drives, turns, brakes or rolls in a seaway. The drone navigates relative to the platform, predicts its motion and the deck's heave, and only lands in a calm moment. One drone or a small fleet: change the speed, wind or sea state, cut GNSS or the radio link, make a vehicle break down, and watch every launch and landing decision explained.

  • 12 scenarios · trucks, ships, robots
  • Relative navigation without GNSS
  • Fleet with reassignment and landing order

AICN: animal intelligence for farms and coasts in Tanzania and Zanzibar

One platform for two jobs: protecting livestock with smart collars, drones and AI, and monitoring sea turtles, dolphins, dugongs and reefs with aerial drones and an underwater robot. Collars keep working without signal and upload later; the AI flags straying, inactive or possibly unwell animals as observations for a farmer or vet to check, never as a diagnosis. Drone missions are planned, checked and approved by an operator. Nest locations stay protected. Sign in as a farmer, a vet, a marine researcher or a drone pilot, make an animal stray or fall ill in the digital twin, and watch the platform notice.

  • Livestock + marine on one platform
  • 10 roles, offline-first data
  • Digital twin with drones and an ROV

Fleet intelligence · Interactive platform

SkyGrid: one operational picture for a whole UAV fleet

A command and coordination platform for many drones at once. Plan an inspection, let SkyGrid check every aircraft against every area and propose the best assignment, approve it, and watch the fleet fly in 2D and 3D with live telemetry. It predicts close approaches with their uncertainty and explains every alert. When a drone's battery fails mid-mission, SkyGrid proposes who takes over its work, and nothing changes until an operator approves. Ten scenarios cover route conflicts, link loss, GNSS faults, a lost aircraft and an aborted mission, and every run ends with a report.

  • Optimal multi-UAV allocation, explained
  • Conflict prediction with uncertainty
  • Human-approved recovery plans

Falconry · Telemetry · Interactive platform

Falcon Intelligence: one record for every falcon

A platform for falcon owners, falconers, breeders and falcon hospitals. Each bird has a digital identity with its documents, weights, feeding, training sessions, veterinary records and lineage. Tracker flights are replayed in 2D and 3D and measured against that bird's own history, never ranked against other birds. Changes in weight or activity are raised as observations for a falconer or vet to check, never as a diagnosis. If a falcon goes missing, a recovery case gathers its last valid position, sightings and an expiring link for helpers. Sign in as an owner, a falconer, a vet or a recovery contact, start a simulated flight, and lose the signal to see what happens.

  • 10 roles, location kept private
  • Every figure labelled by source
  • Imports GPX, KML and CSV tracks
Open the platform

The full backend runs in your browser, nothing to install; the first start downloads about 25 MB and everything resets on reload. Every falcon, record and flight is synthetic, no real tracker is connected yet, and the platform has no trained AI model: its analysis is rules and statistics.
