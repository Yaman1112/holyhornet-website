Layered control architecture

L4 Autonomous soaring
thermal interception, circling (radius from W/R estimate), MacCready glide, energy-aware navigation
L3 Flight control
airspeed-on-pitch, climb/altitude-on-throttle, course→bank, orbit guidance in the air-mass frame (radial PD + curvature feed-forward)
Envelope protection
bank & load-factor limits, stall-margin speed floor, model-based α limiter on elevator, over-speed, stall recovery
L2 Attitude
roll PID, pitch PID, yaw damper, lateral-acceleration turn coordination, aileron-rudder interconnect; gains scheduled with (V_ref/V)²
L1 Actuators
first-order servo lag, rate and deflection limits; throttle lag
Experimental policies (planner variants, future RL) are advisory: their output passes through the deterministic state machine and the envelope; they cannot command actuators.
