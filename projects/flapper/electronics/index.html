Open checks before hardware
  • Pin assignment for every part: from the Matek H743-WLITE documentation only, never assumed.
  • Servo supply voltage: confirm the FC BEC rail matches the ES9051 rating before connecting.
  • Encoder interface: confirm a free SPI bus or timer-capture inputs on the H743-WLITE.
  • Thermal resistance of the motor (6.9 K/W) is an estimate: measure winding temperature on the bench.
  • Gearbox efficiency (0.75) is assumed: measure it; it is the largest loss in the drive.
