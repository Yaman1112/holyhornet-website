Engineering, not slideware

  • • Hungarian assignment with feasibility checks and per-decision explanations
  • • Kalman-filtered ground tracks; uncertainty grows when telemetry stops
  • • CPA, time-to-CPA and infringement probability for every aircraft pair
  • • Equal-area coverage partitioning and zone-avoiding routes
  • • Read-only MAVLink v2 decoder for PX4 and ArduPilot telemetry (CRC-checked)
  • • Role-based access, organisation isolation and a complete audit trail

Safety and honesty by design

SkyGrid is an advisory and coordination system. It is not certified air traffic management and not a detect-and-avoid system.

Planning, simulation and operational modes are separated. Every plan and every recovery needs a human approval, and every decision is recorded.

Airspace data always shows its source and timestamp. Appearing on the map never means permission to fly; national authorisation workflows are configured per country.
