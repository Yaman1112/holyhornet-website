Autonomy · Interactive simulation

Perching UAV: attach, switch off, keep watching

A drone that finds a surface it can hold on to, attaches with switchable magnets or a bar claw, proves the grip will hold, then switches its motors off and works as a sensor station for hours. Before it leaves it checks battery, wind, airspace, motors and the release; if anything fails, it stays attached and reports.

  • 224 W hovering → 2.1 W perched
  • 45 simulated test missions, 0 crashes
  • 10 scenarios with fault injection
  • Research lab: sweeps, Monte Carlo, sensitivity

Counter-UAS · Interactive simulation

SkyShield: see every drone, touch nothing

A passive drone detection and tracking system for protected sites. It fuses radar, RF, Remote ID, camera and acoustic sensors into one airspace picture, classifies each track, and warns the operator about approaches, loitering and zone intrusions. It observes only: no jamming, no spoofing, no interception.

  • 5 sensor types fused
  • 8 scenarios to run
  • Detection only

Soaring autonomy · Research simulator

SkyTherm: a drone that climbs on rising air instead of its battery

A research platform for an autonomous glider that finds thermals and uses them in place of the motor. It has a full 6-degree-of-freedom flight model, an atmosphere with drifting thermals, wind shear and gusts, and on-board estimators that locate a thermal's centre with its uncertainty. A soaring autopilot explains every decision it makes, and a 3-D digital twin replays each flight. Run the milestone and watch the glider find a drifting thermal, centre it and climb, then compare it with plain gliding and motor flight. Research experiments, a published validation report and a separate dynamic-soaring optimiser are included.

  • 6-DOF flight, drifting thermals, wind shear
  • Every autopilot decision explained
  • 17 validation checks, results published
