Questions

Good to know

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us a message

Do I need to buy my own drones?

No. We can fly for you, help you choose and set up your own, or do a mix of both. We'll suggest whatever makes the most sense for your budget.

What happens on the first call?

We ask about the job, the location and what you need at the end of it. You'll leave with a clear idea of what's possible and roughly what it takes.

Which industries do you work with?

Anyone who needs a better view from above, such as energy, construction, security and public safety. See the industries page for more.

Can you train our own team?

Yes. Our education programme helps your people fly safely and get useful data on their own.

Have a job that needs eyes in the air?

Tell us what you're working on. We'll get back to you with honest advice on what it takes.
