How it works

Four simple steps

From first conversation to a system that keeps getting better.

  1. Talk

    Tell us what you need to see, measure or protect, and where.

  2. Plan

    We choose the aircraft, sensors and workflow, and test them in simulation.

  3. Fly

    We set it all up and prove it works in real conditions.

  4. Improve

    We review results with you, train your team and fine-tune over time.

Questions

Good to know

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us a message

Do I need to buy my own drones?

No. We can fly for you, help you choose and set up your own, or do a mix of both. We'll suggest whatever makes the most sense for your budget.

What happens on the first call?

We ask about the job, the location and what you need at the end of it. You'll leave with a clear idea of what's possible and roughly what it takes.

How do I get access to the simulators?

Create a free account on the Simulations page and ask for access. We review each request and let you know when you can open them.

Can you train our own team?

Yes. Our courses cover pilot theory, drone operations and UAV engineering, and we can tailor training to your work.

Have a job that needs eyes in the air?

Tell us what you're working on. We'll get back to you with honest advice on what it takes.
