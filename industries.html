Industries

Drone capability where it matters

We work with organisations across infrastructure, industry, emergency services, farming and technology. If you need a better view from above, we can probably help.

Infrastructure & construction

Progress records, inspection, surveying, site overviews and views of hard-to-reach structures.

Energy & utilities

Inspecting lines, towers, turbines and pipelines, with thermal checks and mapping.

Public safety & emergency

Fast situational awareness, search support and aerial views for responders, where authorised.

Security & counter-drone

Perimeter watch, site awareness and protection against unwanted drones, within the law.

Agriculture

Crop spraying and seed spreading with terrain-following drones that cover big fields fast.

Film, media & production

FPV and stabilised aerial footage for demanding commercial and creative productions.

Logistics & delivery

Heavy-lift drones that carry equipment and supplies to sites that are hard to reach by road.

Research & technology

Prototype aircraft, sensors and integration support for new uses of unmanned systems.

Survey & environment

Mapping land, coastlines and habitats, and monitoring change over time.
